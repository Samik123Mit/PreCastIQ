Nurse-Saul Maturity Index — ACI 306 / ASTM C1074
M = Σ (T − T₀) × Δt
M = Maturity Index (°C·hours)  |  T = Concrete temperature at interval  |  T₀ = −10°C (OPC datum)  |  Δt = Time interval (hours)
